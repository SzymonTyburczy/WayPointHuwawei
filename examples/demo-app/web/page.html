CityRide with Waypoint

A transit app with 20 accessibility defects, and Waypoint running beside it. Everything runs in this page: the Waypoint core as WebAssembly, the React Native screens through react-native-web. Tap the phone the way you would on a device.

Loading the Waypoint core…

Guide asks for a goal, A11y runs the audit, the green microphone takes voice or typed commands.

Try this

  1. Press Guide and type see my past tickets. The guide gives up: the four tabs at the bottom have no names.
  2. Press A11y. Every finding gets a box; press Accept all to apply the suggested labels.
  3. Ask the guide again. It highlights Ticket, then Ticket history; you do the tapping. The baseline cannot tell when it has arrived and keeps going; a model answers done there.
  4. Press the microphone and say or type what is here?, read everything or co tu jest?
